How to play

Your guide to all six games

Every game teaches a different slice of India — its states, its history, its monuments, its words. Here's exactly how each one works, so you can jump straight into playing.

🗺️
Geography

Map Challenge

Name all 28 Indian states from memory before you run out of ideas.

How to play
  1. Type the name of an Indian state into the box and press Guess, or just hit Enter.
  2. Spelling doesn't have to be perfect — capitalisation is ignored, but the name needs to be recognisable.
  3. Every correct guess labels that state on the map immediately.
  4. Keep going until you've named all 28 states, or click Give up to reveal the ones you missed.
  5. Click Play again to clear the map and start a fresh round.

Culture & history

Crossword Challenge

Solve an interlocking crossword built fresh from 80 clues on India's history, culture and geography.

How to play
  1. Enter your name and click Start Game — 12 clues are picked at random and arranged into a real crossword grid.
  2. Click any clue in the Across or Down lists to jump to its word, or click a square directly.
  3. Type letters straight into the grid, and use the arrow keys to move between cells.
  4. Use Check Answers to see how you're doing, and Reveal a Letter if you're stuck.
  5. New Puzzle deals a fresh set of 12 clues any time — a timer and live score track your run.

Quiz

Quiz Challenge

Spin the wheel to pick a topic, then answer against the clock.

How to play
  1. Click Spin to spin the wheel and land on a topic — Geography, History, Polity, Culture, Sports or General Knowledge.
  2. Answer the questions from that topic as they appear.
  3. Landed somewhere you'd rather not? Refresh Wheel resets it for another spin.

Vocabulary

Word Scramble

Unscramble jumbled words across six topics, from wildlife to space missions.

How to play
  1. Pick one of six topics: Science & Tech, Monuments & Architecture, Languages & Literature, Economy & Finance, Environment & Wildlife, or Education & Institutions.
  2. Unscramble the jumbled letters and type your answer — five words per topic.
  3. Stuck? Two hints are available per word, but you only get three hearts (❤️❤️❤️) before the round ends.
  4. Finish the set to see your score, then play again or head back to pick a new topic.

History

Personality Guess

Guess the freedom fighter, one revealed letter at a time.

How to play
  1. A freedom fighter's name is hidden as blanks, with one hint shown about who they are.
  2. Click letters to guess — correct ones fill in the blanks, wrong ones cost you one of six chances.
  3. Guess the full name before six wrong guesses to save the figure and move to the next one.
  4. Progress through Easy, Medium and Hard difficulty — five personalities each, fifteen in total.

Memory

Flip Card Match

Match pairs of India's most iconic monuments.

How to play
  1. Choose a difficulty — Easy (4 pairs), Medium (6 pairs) or Hard (8 pairs).
  2. Flip two cards at a time to reveal the monuments underneath.
  3. Matching pairs stay face-up; a mismatch flips back after a moment, so remember what you've seen.
  4. Match every pair to complete the level, then hit Restart Game to shuffle and try again.
